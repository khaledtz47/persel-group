## Objective and core change

Make a sophisticated business immediately understandable. Let visitors identify their needs before explaining the service ecosystem. Persel's advantage is its knowledge of both organizational hiring and executive career positioning.

Audience A: organizations looking for exceptional executive talent or improved hiring systems.
Audience B: executives seeking their next opportunity, stronger market positioning, leadership development or a fractional career.

The current homepage presents six offers and long explanations together. The rebuilt journey is: identify your audience, find relevant solutions, understand the advantage, see relevant evidence, establish trust, take action.
